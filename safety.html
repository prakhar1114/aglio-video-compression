Safety

Save space without touching fresh memories.

Aglio is designed to help you clean older clutter first, keep control in your hands, and stay private the whole time.

Download Aglio Privacy Policy

On-device by default

Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone. Cleanup logic, compression, and review flows run locally instead of being sent to a cloud service.

Recently Deleted safety net

Removed items still pass through Apple's normal Recently Deleted flow, so cleanup does not feel like a one-way door.

Fresh memories stay out of the way

Use 30, 60, or 90-day style filtering so you focus on older clutter instead of the moments you just captured.

Runs fine in flight mode

Because the core experience runs on-device, Aglio still works without a network connection, even in flight mode.

Data not collected, memories not rushed. Aglio is for people who want to save space carefully, not panic-delete their camera roll.
